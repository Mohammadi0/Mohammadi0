# Adam's Frisör Brand Context

Use this document as the brand and business reference for Shopify theme work performed with the `adams-shopify-redesign` skill.

## Business snapshot

- **Brand:** Adam's Frisör®️
- **Business type:** Independent barbershop and hair salon
- **Location:** Krokstavägen 37, 891 43 Örnsköldsvik, Sweden
- **Website language:** Swedish
- **Primary conversion goal:** Help visitors confidently choose a service and continue to the verified Bokadirekt booking destination.
- **Secondary goals:** Build local trust, present haircut quality, explain services and prices clearly, and make contact and location information easy to find.

## Brand position

Adam's Frisör should feel like a skilled local barber with the polish of a premium Scandinavian studio.

The experience should be:

- Premium but welcoming
- Modern, calm, and precise
- Masculine without looking harsh or heavy
- Personal and trustworthy
- Easy to understand
- Fast to book from, especially on mobile

Avoid making the brand feel like a generic Shopify template, a nightclub, a luxury jewellery shop, or an aggressive streetwear brand.

## Visual identity

### Core colors

- **Charcoal black:** `#1C1C1E`
- **Warm off-white:** `#E8E6E1`

### Supporting accents

Use restrained sand, beige, brass, or muted gold accents only when they improve hierarchy.

Do not cover the interface with gold borders, metallic gradients, glow effects, or decorative luxury symbols.

### Color use

- Charcoal works well for navigation, dark sections, cards, and strong contrast.
- Warm off-white should create breathing room and a softer Scandinavian appearance.
- Accent colors should guide attention to selected details, not compete with the booking CTA.
- Maintain readable text contrast and visible focus states.
- Do not rely on color alone to communicate selection, error, availability, or state.

## Typography

- Prefer the theme's existing typography system and font controls.
- Use a clean, editorial hierarchy with clear differences between headings, body text, labels, and prices.
- Headings may feel confident and refined, but they must remain highly readable.
- Avoid very thin weights, excessive letter spacing, long uppercase paragraphs, and body text below a comfortable mobile size.
- Do not introduce external font dependencies unless explicitly approved.

## Photography direction

Use real haircut photography as the strongest visual proof.

Preferred image treatment:

- Matte charcoal or clean neutral backgrounds
- Soft cinematic overhead lighting with subtle front fill
- Natural skin texture and proportions
- Realistic hair texture, fade transitions, and blend work
- Controlled contrast and natural color
- Minimal visual distractions
- Premium editorial barbershop presentation

Avoid:

- Plastic-looking skin
- Artificially rebuilt hairlines or haircut shapes
- Oversaturation
- Strong filters that hide haircut detail
- Generic stock barber imagery when real salon work is available
- Heavy image overlays that reduce clarity

## Customer-facing voice

All visible storefront copy should be written in natural Swedish.

The tone should be:

- Confident
- Clear
- Professional
- Friendly
- Brief enough to scan quickly
- Helpful without pressuring the visitor

Use concrete language such as:

- `Boka tid`
- `Se tjänster och priser`
- `Hjälp mig välja`
- `Hitta till salongen`
- `Se fler klippningar`

Never use the English word `grooming` in Swedish storefront copy.

Avoid exaggerated claims such as `Sveriges bästa barberare`, guaranteed results, unsupported awards, or artificial urgency.

## Customer journey priorities

A first-time visitor should quickly understand:

1. What Adam's Frisör offers
2. Where the salon is located
3. Which service may fit their needs
4. What the service costs or starts from
5. How to book through Bokadirekt
6. Why the salon is trustworthy

The preferred navigation is:

- Hem
- Tjänster & priser
- Hjälp mig välja
- Galleri
- Om oss
- Kontakt

The primary CTA label is `Boka tid`.

Do not present many competing primary actions in the same viewport. Supporting links may be visually quieter.

## Booking rules

- Preserve verified Bokadirekt links already present in the theme or merchant settings.
- Never invent Bokadirekt service IDs or direct service URLs.
- When a verified direct service link is unavailable, use the verified main booking page.
- External booking links should be clearly distinguishable from internal navigation.
- Booking actions should remain visible and easy to reach on small screens.
- Do not replace Bokadirekt with an unapproved booking system.

## Local SEO context

Use local phrases naturally where they genuinely describe the page:

- `barberare Örnsköldsvik`
- `herrklippning Örnsköldsvik`
- `frisör Örnsköldsvik`
- `skin fade`
- `skäggtrimning`

Do not repeat locations or keywords unnaturally. Preserve the theme's existing metadata, canonical URL, social sharing, and structured-data systems unless a verified issue requires a focused change.

## Recommended homepage hierarchy

A strong homepage may include:

1. Hero with one clear booking CTA
2. Compact trust bar
3. Featured services
4. Help choosing a service
5. Why choose Adam's Frisör
6. Haircut gallery
7. Simple booking steps
8. Customer reviews
9. Location and opening hours
10. Final booking CTA

This is a direction, not a requirement to replace useful native theme sections. Extend existing architecture whenever practical.

## Theme Editor expectations

The merchant should be able to edit or reorder relevant content without touching code.

Prefer settings and blocks for:

- Headings and descriptions
- Images and alt text
- CTA labels and links
- Services and prices
- Reviews
- Benefits and trust points
- Gallery items
- Steps
- FAQs
- Contact details

Do not hardcode content that belongs in a section setting, block, menu, page, metafield, or existing global theme setting.

## Non-negotiables

- Do not publish or overwrite the live Shopify theme without explicit permission.
- Do not remove existing integrations without understanding their purpose.
- Do not introduce a separate CSS or JavaScript framework.
- Do not invent reviews, ratings, awards, booking links, service IDs, or business claims.
- Do not sacrifice readability or booking speed for decorative effects.
- Do not alter merchant content unnecessarily when a focused reusable component solves the request.
